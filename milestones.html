Project Proposal

Completed

The group presents the research idea, the literature survey, the gap and the proposed solution to a panel, and submits the proposal report. The panel decides whether the scope and the individual components are suitable.

Date
16–18 March 2026
Marks allocated
12% (presentation and report)
Proposal submission
15 March 2026
Assessed as
Group and individual

What is assessed

  • Topic assessment and project charter
  • Proposal presentation (group and individual components)
  • Proposal report

Progress Presentation 1

Completed

Demonstrates that roughly half of the implementation is complete. Each member presents the design, dataset and first working model for their component.

Date
11–13 May 2026
Marks allocated
15%
Checklist submission
13 May 2026
Assessed as
Group and individual

What is assessed

  • Working prototype of each component
  • Dataset collection and preprocessing evidence
  • Initial model results

Progress Presentation 2

Completed

Demonstrates a near-complete, integrated system. The focus moves from individual models to the end-to-end inspection flow, model comparisons and testing.

Date
31 August – 2 September 2026
Marks allocated
18%
Assessed as
Group and individual

What is assessed

  • Integrated mobile app and four backend services
  • Model comparison experiments and results
  • Testing evidence and deployment

Final Assessment

Upcoming

Date
19–21 October 2026
Marks allocated
20% (final presentation and viva), 19% (final report)
Final thesis submission
28 October 2026
Assessed as
Group and individual

What is assessed

  • Final report: group document and individual documents
  • Research paper
  • Project website

Viva

Upcoming

An individual oral examination in which each member is questioned on their own component, the decisions behind it and their contribution to the group work.

Date
19–21 October 2026
Marks allocated
20% (combined with the final presentation)
Assessed as
Individual

What is assessed

  • Individual oral examination
  • Defence of the individual research component
