SLIIT Research Project · R26-IT-071

Know what a used vehicle is really worth before you buy it.

Watinakama.LK is an AI-powered vehicle inspection and valuation system. With nothing more than a smartphone, a buyer can check a vehicle's identity, body condition and engine health, and receive a fair, evidence-based market price.

Explore the research Documents Meet the team

Abstract

Buying a second-hand vehicle in Sri Lanka depends heavily on trust. Sellers know far more about a vehicle than buyers do, prices are set by negotiation rather than evidence, and an independent inspection needs a mechanic, a body-shop expert and time that most buyers do not have. Tampered chassis numbers, concealed accident damage and early-stage engine faults are routinely missed until after the sale.

Watinakama.LK addresses this with four cooperating machine-learning components delivered through one mobile application. A convolutional network verifies whether the VIN plate is original or altered. A set of lightweight image classifiers inspects five views of the exterior and produces a Body Condition Score. An audio pipeline built on YAMNet embeddings and a support vector machine listens to the engine and produces a Mechanical Health Score.

Finally, a stacking ensemble combines these three condition signals with the vehicle's specifications to estimate a fair market value and to tell the buyer whether the asking price is a good deal, fair, or overpriced.

What is new is the integration. Existing work treats damage detection, engine diagnosis and price prediction as separate problems, and existing price models rely on listing data alone. This project links measured condition to price in a single workflow that a non-expert can complete in minutes.

Four research components, one inspection

Each component is an independent research contribution with its own model, dataset and API. Together they form the inspection workflow.

1

VIN Authentication

Classifies a photographed VIN plate as Original, Altered or Needs Review using a fine-tuned ResNet50.

2

Body Condition Analysis

Analyses front, rear, left, right and roof images for damage and returns a Body Condition Score from 0 to 100.

3

Engine Sound Diagnosis

Detects knocking, misfiring, imbalance, tappet noise and starting faults from a phone recording, with a Mechanical Health Score.

4

Market Valuation

Combines specifications with the three condition results to predict a fair price and a buy / negotiate verdict.

How an inspection works

  1. Enter vehicle details. Make, model, year, mileage and the seller's asking price.
  2. Scan the VIN plate. The app checks the photo and the VIN model returns its verdict.
  3. Photograph five views. Each photo is validated against the expected view before damage analysis.
  4. Record the engine. Three short recordings: start, idle and acceleration.
  5. Read the report. Component scores, a fair market value, a negotiation range and a verdict.
