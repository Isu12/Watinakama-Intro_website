About the project

Watinakama.LK takes its name from the Sinhala word for value. The project is the group's final-year research project for the BSc (Hons) in Information Technology at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology.

Development began in April 2026. The code is developed in the open on GitHub, with each model service packaged as its own Docker container.

Achievements

  • Four model services and an API gateway, each packaged as a Docker container.
  • End-to-end mobile inspection flow working against the live services.
  • Research paper in preparation, due for submission on 23 October 2026.

Watinakama.LK

An AI-powered vehicle inspection and valuation system for the Sri Lankan second-hand vehicle market. Undergraduate research project R26-IT-071, Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT).
